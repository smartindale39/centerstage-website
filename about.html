About Us

Owner & Director

Miss April

Owner & Director

Since attending her first dance class in 1973, April fell in love with the art of dance. She trained with multiple choreographers from New York City throughout her teenage years. Her greatest achievements include taking Tap classes from Gregory Hines and Savion Glover.

At age 16 she was offered a teaching position at a local dance school, and never looked back. She earned a BA in Mathematics and an MS in Elementary Education from SCSU — all while continuing to dance and teach.

April continues working full time as a Math teacher in East Haven, raising her family with her husband Danny, and teaching dance every moment she can.

Our Story

Established in 2015, Center Stage Dance Academy has always been about dance — the empowerment of it, the beauty of the movements, and the freedom of self-expression.

We pride ourselves in being nonparticipants in dance competitions. The focus is on the show.

Every spring, after months of hard work perfecting their craft, our students put on a wondrous recital. Uniquely themed for each new rendition, students of all ages showcase everything they have learned throughout the year.

Center Stage and its teachers are committed to extending their love for dance to every willing person who walks through our doors. We welcome youths of all backgrounds and experience levels — come find out what dance is all about.
