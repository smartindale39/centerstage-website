About Us

Owner & Director

Miss April

Owner & Director

Since attending her first dance class in 1973, April fell in love with the art of dance. She trained with multiple choreographers from New York City throughout her teenage years, with her greatest achievements including Tap classes from Gregory Hines and Savion Glover.

At age 16 she was offered a teaching position at a local dance school, and never looked back. She earned a BA in Mathematics and an MS in Elementary Education from SCSU — all while continuing to dance and teach.

April continues working full time as a Math teacher in East Haven, raising her family with her husband Danny, and teaching dance every moment she can.

Our Story

Established in 2015, Center Stage Dance Academy has always been about dance — the empowerment of it, the beauty of the movements, and the freedom of self-expression.

We pride ourselves in being nonparticipants in dance competitions. The focus is on the show.

Every spring our students put on a wondrous recital — uniquely themed, showcasing everything they have worked toward throughout the year. We welcome youths of all backgrounds and experience levels. Come find out what dance is all about.

What We Believe

Everyone Belongs

No experience required. We welcome students of all ages and backgrounds from the very first class.

Joy Over Competition

We are proudly competition-free. Our energy goes into the craft, the community, and the annual recital.

Expert Instruction

Our teachers bring decades of professional training and a genuine passion for passing it on.

The Show

Every spring our students take the stage. The recital is the moment everything comes together.
