Center Stage Dance Academy is a studio dedicated to teaching correct dance technique, theory, and terminology for beginning through advanced students of all ages. We do not believe in over-inflated prices or excessive performances.

We believe in teaching the basics of the art form to provide a solid foundation — both mentally and physically — that enables each student to understand and perform dance at any level and appreciate the true beauty of the art form.

Our teaching staff consists of vibrant and energetic instructors who understand what it takes to be a great dancer and make it a priority to pass that knowledge on. Our students are our first priority.

Class Structure

All classes begin with barre work or center warm-ups — the basis of the specific dance technique being learned. Each step in the warm-up is used later in center work or across-the-floor combinations. New students will be placed in the lower level class according to age, with ability assessed and advancement at the discretion of the teacher.

Acro Placement Levels

  • Acro 1 — Beginning level, ages 5 and older
  • Acro 2 — Must perform a standing backbend and cartwheel
  • Acro 3 — Must perform a standing backbend and pull up independently, a back walkover, and hold a handstand

If you have any questions or concerns about your child's placement, please contact the studio directly.

Proper attire is essential — instructors must be able to see body lines to ensure correct form and technique.

Ages 5 & Under

  • Any colored leotard & tights
  • Skirt (attached or separate)
  • Dance bags brought into the dance room
  • Shoes changed in the dance room by teachers
  • Parents are not allowed to enter the dance room without permission

Ages 6 & Up

  • Any colored leotard or unitard
  • Leggings or shorts
  • Crop tops or fitted tops
  • No sweatshirts or hoodies
  • Dance bags left in the waiting area
  • No outside street shoes on the dance floor
  • Shoes changed in the waiting area
  • Only water bottles allowed in the dance room
  • Phones must remain in dance bag in the waiting area

All Students

  • Hair must be in a high ponytail, braids, or bun before entering class — short hair pulled back from the face
  • Students may not leave the dance room without permission

Center Stage Dance Academy provides a 30-week curriculum divided into 9 monthly payments. Tuition is due the first week of each month and is based on the 9-month season from September to May. Costumes for the annual recital are included in monthly tuition.

Payment Policies

  • We do not provide monthly invoices — it is the account holder's responsibility to pay on time
  • Tuition is considered delinquent after the 15th of each month — a $20 late fee will be applied
  • Accounts more than 60 days delinquent may result in suspension of lessons
  • Accounts must be current before students may receive costumes or participate in performances
  • Returned checks are subject to a $25 fee — all subsequent payments must be made in cash

Accepted Payment Methods

  • Cash or Check
  • PayPal
  • Venmo
  • Studio Pro Parent Portal

Our primary goal is for students to have fun while learning dance technique and strong work ethics. We expect all dancers to show respect to themselves, each other, and all instructors.

In the Studio

  • Arrive on time and prepared with proper attire and shoes
  • Hair must be pulled up before entering class
  • No cell phones in the dance room
  • No gum in class
  • Only water bottles allowed in the dance room
  • Do not talk during class unless asking a question
  • No sitting during class
  • Do not leave the dance room without permission
  • Clap at the end of class to thank the instructor
  • No running, yelling, or rough play in the studio
  • If injured, notify the instructor before class
  • If sick, do not come to class
  • If leaving early, notify the instructor before class begins

Respect & Community

  • Be respectful and kind to instructors, peers, and staff at all times
  • No obscene language or inappropriate conversations — by parents or dancers
  • Gossiping among students or parents will not be tolerated
  • Derogatory comments about the studio, staff, or fellow students — in person, by phone, or on social media — are strictly prohibited
  • The use of alcohol and/or drugs is strictly forbidden on studio premises

All students and parents understand that Miss April has the final say on placement and all matters concerning Center Stage Dance Academy. The studio reserves the right to dismiss any student due to inappropriate or disruptive behavior.
