Classes

How It Works

Classes at Center Stage Dance Academy are created based on age and ability. Every dancer is placed in the environment where they will thrive most. Class sizes, times, and dates vary based on enrollment each year. Reach out or visit the Parent Portal for current scheduling information.

Our Offerings

The perfect first dance experience. This combo class introduces our youngest dancers to the fundamentals of both Ballet and Tap in a single fun, welcoming session. Students begin to develop coordination, rhythm, and a love for movement in a nurturing environment.

Age Duration Shoes
2.5 to 5 years 45 min Pink/White Ballet + Tan Tap

A classical ballet class that teaches the formalized technique of dance, including specific rules regarding the positions of the arms, feet, and body. Choreography is built on combinations of fundamental movements that form the foundation for all dance styles. Students develop posture, discipline, and grace.

Age Duration Shoes
7 to 8 years 30 min Pink or White

A rhythmic dance style where the sounds of tap shoes striking the floor become the music. Students learn to use their feet as percussion instruments, developing a strong sense of timing, musicality, and coordination. A timeless and uniquely satisfying style of dance.

Age Duration Shoes
7 years and up 30 min Tan Mary Janes

A technical and high-energy dance style performed to popular music. Students build strength and flexibility while working on turns, leaps, and dance technique. Jazz is an ever-evolving style that challenges dancers to be expressive, sharp, and precise.

Age Duration Shoes
7 to 8 years 30 min Tan Jazz Boots
8 years and up 45 min Tan Jazz Boots

A high-energy street style of dance performed in sneakers. Less formal than classical styles, Hip Hop focuses on rhythm, personality, and self-expression. Students learn combinations inspired by music videos and popular culture in an upbeat, welcoming environment.

Age Duration Shoes
7 to 8 years 30 min Sneakers
9 years and up 45 min Sneakers

Tumbling takes center stage in this jazz-based class. Students work on acrobatic skills including somersaults, backbends, cartwheels, handstands, and more, all integrated into dynamic jazz choreography. Placement is by skill level. We recommend taking an additional dance class alongside this one.

Age Duration Shoes
5 to 6 years 30 min Barefoot
7 years and up 45 min Barefoot

A fusion of ballet, jazz, and contemporary technique. Lyrical Ballet is smoother and more expressive than traditional ballet but not as fast-paced as jazz. Students use movement to interpret music and emotion, developing fluidity, extension, and artistic expression alongside strong technical foundations.

Age Duration Shoes
8 years and up 45 min Pink or White

A theatrical dance style that fuses ballet, jazz, and modern technique with acting and storytelling. Students perform to songs from Broadway and musicals, bringing characters and emotions to life through movement. An ideal class for students who love performance and want to combine dance with dramatic expression.

Age Duration Shoes
8 years and up 45 min Tan Character (1.5" heel)

Solo, Duo & Trio

For our most dedicated and experienced dancers, we offer individual and small group performance opportunities. Placement is based on total years of dance training, regardless of where those years were spent.

Solo

15+ total years of dance

An individual performance tailored to the dancer's strengths and style. Our most advanced offering for dancers with a deep foundation of training.

Duo

10+ total years of dance

A partnered performance combining two dancers in a shared routine. Requires strong individual technique and the ability to move in sync with a partner.

Trio

5+ total years of dance

A small group performance for three dancers. A great step into collaborative performance for students with a solid foundation in dance.
